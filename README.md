## 🔄 The "Infinite Free" Rotation Strategy

How do you develop software indefinitely without hitting paywalls or degrading context quality? Use this pipeline rotation:

1. **Bootstrap** with [Google Antigravity](https://antigravity.google/) or [Firebase Studio](https://idx.dev/) for your heavy, architectural development environment.
2. **Scaffold** your initial UI components rapidly with [Bolt.new](https://bolt.new/) or [Lovable](https://lovable.dev/).
3. **Hit a rate limit?** Immediately export the repository state to [Windsurf](https://codeium.com/windsurf) or VS Code (running [Cline](https://github.com/cline/cline)).
4. **Need API Keys?** Route requests through [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/) for free Gemini 1.5 Pro keys to power your BYOK terminal tools.
5. **Need Compute?** Offload model training or heavy data scripts to [Colab](https://colab.research.google.com/) or [Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/code).
6. **Debugging roadblocks?** Paste complex tracebacks into [Phind](https://www.phind.com/) or [Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai/).
7. **Need massive context?** Switch to [Tongyi Lingma](https://tongyi.aliyun.com/lingma/) or [Trae](https://www.trae.ai/) when you need an agent to ingest your entire repository at once without cost.

> **Disclaimer:** Pricing architectures and models iterate rapidly. Tiers are accurate as of November 21, 2025. Always audit a tool's official pricing page before baking it into your core workflow.
